# Privacy Policy — Badges

_Last updated: 20 September 2026_

**Badges collects no data.**

Badges is a macOS Finder extension that draws file-type badges on files in Finder. It
is designed to work entirely on your Mac.

## What we collect

Nothing. Badges does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or
usage information.

## Network

Badges makes **no network connections**. It has no analytics, no tracking, no
advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

## Your data stays on your device

Your settings — the badge rules, categories, custom badge images, and on/off state —
are stored **locally on your Mac** (in the app's sandboxed container / App Group) so the
app and its Finder extension can share them. This data never leaves your device and is
not accessible to us.

Badges reads only the file names/extensions of items shown in Finder in order to choose
which badge to display. It does not read, open, upload, or transmit the contents of your
files.

## Children

Badges is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
